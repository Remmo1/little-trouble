WELCOME TO THE ARTILLERY CLUB

Make a little trouble.

Gather a friend, recruit some bots, or mix both. Three worms each. One gloriously destructible meadow.

Same-computer multiplayer + bots work in this preview. For LAN play, run the included local server, open its address in your browser, and choose LAN play. Players on the same network can then join your room.

FIELD GUIDE

Be the last team standing.

Each team has three worms with 100 HP. Teams alternate turns; your living worms take turns automatically. Friendly fire is very real.

01 · Get in position

Hold A / D or the Move buttons. Press J to jump. You have 100 movement points each turn. Falling too far hurts; water is lethal.

02 · Line it up

Click or tap the sky to aim, or use the angle slider. 90° is straight up; angles above 90° face left. Set your power and watch the wind.

03 · Make a crater

Bazookas explode on impact. Grenades bounce and explode after 3 seconds. Both destroy terrain and can knock worms into water.

04 · Pass it on

Fire once to end your turn, or choose Skip turn. A dashed guide shows the start of your arc, not a guaranteed landing point.

Local games pause while a dialog is open. LAN games keep running unless the host pauses the match in the lobby. Sounds are optional and start only after you enable them.
